HOW TO USE THE LOGO
-------------------
- Use the primary logo on light backgrounds and the reverse logo on dark ones.
- Keep clear space around the logo equal to at least the height of the "T"
  in the wordmark on all sides.
- Minimum digital width: 120 px. Minimum print width: 30 mm.
- Do not stretch, rotate, recolour, outline or add effects to the logo.
- Do not place the logo on a busy or low-contrast background.
- Do not use the ToolsTrek name or logo in a way that suggests endorsement,
  partnership or sponsorship without written permission.
